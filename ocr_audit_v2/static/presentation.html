LoRA setup and observed gain

Qwen3.5-9B · rank 32 · alpha 64 · dropout 0.05 · learning rate 1e-4 · one epoch · batch 1 with accumulation 8 · bfloat16 · language and vision modules. No validation-based checkpoint selection.

On the recorded 407 development sample: matched items 81/121 before LoRA and 85/121 after it; correct identifiers 27/32 and 30/32. A modest sample gain, not a general accuracy claim.

The verification gate

Deterministic checks on every page

  • Identifier formats (receipt, order, invoice)
  • Quantity × price = net
  • Commission matches the configured rule (4% in this corpus)
  • Line total = net + commission + VAT
  • Sum of lines = printed total

Decision

All page checks pass → eligible for document matching
Any check fails → referred with the reason

Targeted re-read

Only suspicious pages are read again with different image settings. Large bundles where most pages look suspicious are referred directly: that can indicate an unfamiliar template or weak extraction.

Matching is line by line (item, quantity, price). The invoice is counted once; ambiguous allocations are referred.

Duplicate receipts

  • Within a bundle: a receipt number that reappears after another receipt
  • Across bundles: in the competition check
  • The note shows both pages, and the verdict is held for the reviewer

Batch scope: extra invoice lines may belong to receipts outside the uploaded batch. A competition-wide absence is assessed only after grouping all its bundles.
